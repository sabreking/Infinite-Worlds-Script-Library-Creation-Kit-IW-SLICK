Step 2 — Items to track

Items to track are the notebooks the game keeps between turns. This step tells you which notebooks this look needs.

Why these notebooks

Every turn, Infinite Worlds draws a brand new picture. It does not keep the last picture. This look also throws away last turn’s written description of the face and the clothes. It does that by putting the turn number on the subject line and by marking the clothes as changed. The notebooks are the written description the new picture copies. A notebook here means an Items to track entry. Without it, the face drifts, because there is nothing stable to copy.

If your cast is small, add a row when someone speaks or is physically in the scene. If your cast is large, add a row only when the Relationship Tracker marks that person significant: true. That word is a yes-or-no flag on their relationship row. A large cast therefore also needs the Relationship set on the Notebooks page. If that set is missing, the flag never gets set, and the appearance list stays empty.

Always copy the player notebook. Then copy either the few-people list or the many-people list. Copy one of those NPC lists, not both. Two lists for the same people will disagree about how they look.

Relationship, intimacy, and the NPC appearance update are a separate set. Open the Notebooks page after the pictures are working, and copy that set there.

Step 3 — Fill blanks on what you pasted

Some notes in the script are wrapped in <# … #>. The game skips everything between those marks. The author hid optional packs there — sex scenes, fights, and long clothing rules — so you do not pay for them every turn until you want them. When you want a pack, fill any square brackets inside it, then delete the <# and the #>. When this page tells you to leave a wrap closed, leave it closed.

  • Search the text you pasted for notes wrapped in <# … #>. The game skips those notes until you delete the wrap.
  • Find the line with square brackets for period, region, and the kind of artist. Write your choices. Then delete the wrap so the game can read that line.
  • Find the mood line. Write three to five mood words. Then delete the wrap around that line too.
  • Sex pose packs and fight pose packs stay wrapped unless your game actually shows those scenes. Opening one sends it every turn.

Step 4 — Image style

These four boxes are a blank template for the words that wrap the picture. Paste them into the Image style prefix and suffix fields, then keep or delete paragraphs so they match this look.

Those fields go to the picture model only. They do not go to the story writer. That is why you should not paste them into the story Description field. Description is how the story talks. Image style is how the picture looks.

A named style from the Styles page and this blank template are two different wrappers. Use one of them. If you paste both, the picture model receives two looks at once and they fight.

If you would rather start from a finished look, open Styles, pick one, and copy its prefix and its suffix instead of these four boxes.

Keep or delete for this look

  • Keep the words about paint, brushes, and illustration.
  • Delete camera and film words, and delete Unreal Engine lines, unless you truly want the painting to look like a game screenshot.
  • Delete the paragraph that is only for manga or comics.
