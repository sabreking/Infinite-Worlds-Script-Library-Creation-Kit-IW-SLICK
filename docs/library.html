THE SCRIPT LIBRARY 3.29

Pick one look.

Open one picture look and stay on that page until the pictures in your game look right. The chip on the card is the exact name of the Extra Instruction Block, which is the extra rule sheet you paste. Copy the whole page. Turn on one look only.

Why you pick one look

Every turn, Infinite Worlds draws a brand new picture. It does not reuse the last picture. What it can reuse is the written description of a person’s face and clothes, and only when you spell their name the same way every time. If that description is vague, the new picture invents a new face even though the name matches. That saved description is the picture cache.

Wrangler and Bareback keep that saved description. That is why their cards say you do not create an appearance notebook. Every other look throws the saved description away on purpose, so you keep notebooks the new picture can copy.

Turn on one picture script. Every block you leave enabled is sent on every turn, which costs more, and these scripts all write the same picture fields. If two of them are on, they contradict each other and the picture comes out muddled.

Stay on that look’s page until the pictures are in your game and they look right. You can come back later for story voice, notebooks, or a named picture style.
