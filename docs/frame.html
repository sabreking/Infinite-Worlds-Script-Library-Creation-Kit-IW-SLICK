Words around the picture.

You do not need this page to finish a look. Each look page already includes these four boxes as step 4. A finished named look lives on Styles. Use that named look or this blank template. Do not paste both, or the picture model receives two looks at once.

What to keep and what to delete

  • Photographs. Keep the tone and the lines that sound like photos or computer graphics. Delete the paragraph that is only for manga or comics. That paragraph would pull a photograph toward a comic page.
  • A painting. Keep the words about paint, brushes, and illustration. Delete camera and film words. Delete Unreal Engine lines unless you truly want the painting to look like a game screenshot.
  • Manga pages. Keep the manga and comic paragraph. That is the page layout you picked. Delete Unreal Engine lines, which push the comic toward a game screenshot.
  • A game that does not show sex in the pictures. Delete the phrases about sexually stylized art, erotic styling, and genitalia. Those words are instructions to the picture model. Leaving them in will push the pictures that way.
